Everything you need to stay on track

A small set of tools that work well together.

Kanban board

See the work in three columns and drag a card to the next one when it moves on.

List view

Prefer rows? Switch to a list and change a task status with one click.

Priorities

Mark tasks as low, normal, high or urgent and filter the board by priority.

Due dates

Give a task a deadline. Overdue and upcoming dates are highlighted for you.

Separate projects

Keep each project apart, with its own colour, description and progress bar.

Private by default

You sign in with your own account and nobody else can open your projects.
